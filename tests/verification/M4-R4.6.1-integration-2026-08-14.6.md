## Addressed reviewer findings

### M4-UX-R11: responsive result plots

- The in-app comparison and transmittance panels are separate responsive plot
  outputs. Panel B remains narrower and to the right at 1400 px and above, then
  stacks below panel A at narrower widths.
- Both panels receive their own title, panel tag, accessible description, and
  complete plotting area.
- The comparison plot uses inset endpoint expansion and explicit 380 and 780 nm
  endpoint ticks. The same margins apply to current and archived PNG exports.
- The single-panel legend uses two rows and additional right margin.

### M4-UX-R12: upload preview proximity

- Flexible CSV settings remain available as a disclosure.
- The disclosure is collapsed by default on production Transmission tabs, so
  the persistent preview and readiness feedback follow the active upload task
  without the full parser form intervening.
- The isolated development module retains expanded parser settings for fixture
  and parser testing.

### M4-UX-R13: German active-state label

- The history selector and archive controls use the localized active suffix.
- German now displays `(aktiv)` and English continues to display `(active)`.

### M4-UX-R14: keyboard-operated downloads

- Every enabled current and archived download is a native visible button linked
  to its Shiny download handler.
- Enter activates each download exactly once. Space is also supported because
  the visible control is a button.
- Focus remains on the initiating button after activation, and disabled states
  remain actual disabled buttons.
- Hidden Shiny download targets are kept active even when their containing tab
  is not visible.

### M4-UX-R15: Transmission shared-ID warnings

- Display-only `gt` tables are rendered as raw `gt` HTML through ordinary Shiny
  output bindings.
- This avoids `gt::gt_output()` registering both an input and output under the
  same identifier.
- Preview, current-result, history, and archived-result outputs now have one
  production binding per identifier. Diagnostics are not suppressed.

Scientific calculations, normalization rules, catalogue provenance, history
semantics, CSV exports, and audit ZIP contracts are unchanged.
