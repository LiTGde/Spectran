## Addressed finding

- `M2-UX-R06`: The information tooltip now begins directly at the trigger
  boundary, removing the pointer gap that previously caused it to disappear
  before the pointer could enter the visible content.
- The visible tooltip accepts pointer hover, so moving from the icon into the
  explanatory content keeps it visible.
- Escape adds a dismissed state to the tooltip wrapper while the native info
  button retains keyboard focus. The dismissed state overrides both focus and
  hover display.
- Moving focus away removes the dismissed state, so later focus or hover shows
  the tooltip again. Clicking the info button also re-arms it.
- The interaction uses `shinyjs::onevent()`, `addClass()`, and `removeClass()`
  from Spectran's existing dependency. No bespoke JavaScript or new dependency
  was added.

No tooltip wording, readiness rule, normalization, Apply behavior, scientific
calculation, metric definition, snapshot, or export contract changed.
