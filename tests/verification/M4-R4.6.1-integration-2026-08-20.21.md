## Scope

This focused revision implements two user-requested presentation refinements.
Scientific calculations, metric identifiers, CSV output, and audit data are
unchanged.

## Changes

- When the lighter-incident-fill option is selected, the incident spectrum is
  drawn with the established Analysis layer order: the full spectral colour
  layer, a semi-transparent white overlay, and then the dashed incident line.
  The transmitted spectrum retains the saturated spectral fill and solid line.
- Visible metric symbols in all three `gt` result-table groups now use
  typographic subscripts. For example, `τv,D65` is rendered as
  τ<sub>v,D65</sub>. The underlying plain-text symbols remain unchanged for
  machine-readable exports and audit records.
