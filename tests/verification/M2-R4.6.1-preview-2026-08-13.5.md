  Enter and Space as well as pointer activation.

## Browser verification

- All five help controls were tested at 768 px in both collapsed and expanded
  states. Enter and Space each opened and closed every control exactly once.
- Pointer activation was repeated on the passive-filter help and continued to
  open and close the same labelled region while retaining focus.
- Focus stayed on the activated button after every transition. Its accessible
  name changed between Collapsed and Expanded.
- The page position remained unchanged for individual Enter and Space
  activation across all five controls at 320, 390, 768, 1024, 1200, and
  1440 px, for 30 control-width combinations.
- All five help regions were also expanded together at each width. Buttons and
  panels remained inside the viewport, and no page-level horizontal overflow
  occurred.
- Every help button is in the ordinary document focus order with `tabindex=0`.
  Browser-control limitations prevented an independent automated Tab movement
  assertion, so the reviewer should repeat Tab and Shift+Tab traversal.
- Browser diagnostics contained no warning or error entries.
