M4-UX-R04: Phone lightbox does not provide meaningful image enlargement
State: open; medium-priority acceptance revision.

Reproduction:
- DE 320 x 844, Introduction, open Lichtpfad through its 'vergroessern' button.
- EN 390 x 844, open Material selection or Table Export through 'enlarge image'.

Observed:
The lightbox only fits the entire screenshot into the phone width. Its displayed
image is approximately the same size as the already full-column thumbnail, and
the 320-px light-path sample is slightly smaller. Fine interface labels, chart
labels and table text cannot be read comfortably. There is no visible control to
enlarge further or inspect image detail. Double-clicking the German light-path
image produces no visible change. Closing and focus behavior still work.

Impact:
The preview action promises enlargement but adds no useful detail access on a
phone. This is a regression in the purpose of the full-size preview, even though
the new modal itself opens and closes correctly. Browser-level pinch/zoom was
not tested and is not being claimed unavailable.

Requested outcome:
Retain the fitted overview and add an evident, keyboard-operable way to inspect
larger/native-size detail inside the same lightbox. Keep any image scrolling
within the viewer, keep close controls reachable, avoid page-wide overflow, and
preserve Escape, focus containment and return to the opening thumbnail. A simple
fit/detail toggle is sufficient; no new tab, asset set or gallery redesign is
needed. Recheck only this correction and its adjacent dialog behavior.
