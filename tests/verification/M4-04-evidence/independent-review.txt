M4-UX-R02: Collapsed navigation leaves invisible keyboard targets
State: closed by visible evidence.
Reproduction: close the desktop sidebar or use the closed mobile menu, focus

returns to Toggle navigation. Reopening restores the links to keyboard operation;
Enter on the material item opens the material module. The expanded item retains
two lines and separate Transmission and Reflection/Reflexion icons.
