## Addressed reviewer finding

### M4-UX-R16: German tabs did not switch their visible panels

- Shiny generated the same short random `data-tabsetid` for the German Import
  and Transmission tabsets.
- Their first three Bootstrap panel targets therefore had duplicate document
  IDs. Selecting Spectrum, Measurement, or Normalization could activate the
  matching hidden Import panel while leaving the Transmission body unchanged.
- Transmission now assigns deterministic panel targets derived from the
  globally namespaced Shiny input ID.
- The main Transmission tabset, current metric-table tabset, and archived
  metric-table tabset all use the collision-free helper.
- Every navigation target and panel ID remains unique even when other tabsets
  are rendered on the same page.

Scientific calculations, normalization, imports, catalogue data, Apply,
promotion, history, plots, tables, downloads, and audit exports are unchanged.
M4-UX-R11 through M4-UX-R15 remain unchanged from the accepted `.6` surfaces.
