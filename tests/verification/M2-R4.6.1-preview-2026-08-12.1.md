## Browser verification

The isolated app was exercised in the Codex in-app browser with local,
deterministic fixtures.

- Initial state: three readiness actions; Apply disabled.
- Selective filter with D65 at 250 lx: Apply enabled; 6 D65 property rows, 12
  absolute/retained rows, and 10 action-factor/DER change rows rendered.
- Calculation edit after Apply: previous snapshot remained visible, was muted
  to opacity 0.52, was labelled out of date for assistive technology, and
  future promotion/download gates became false.
- Re-Apply: snapshot became current and metric values updated.
- Active source change: snapshot became stale until Re-Apply.
- Zero source: absolute values remained zero while all affected retained,
  action-factor, and DER ratios showed `Undefined` with visible warnings.
- Keyboard: Enter activated Apply and advanced the Apply sequence.
- Browser diagnostics: no warning or error entries.

Responsive checks used 320, 390, 768, 1024, 1200, and 1440 px widths. None
produced page-level horizontal overflow. Wide metric tables used their own
scrollable regions at 320, 390, and where needed at 1200 px. The metric columns
stacked through 1024 px and returned to 5/7 columns at 1200 px. Plots stayed
inside the page at all checked widths.

## Intended review path

1. Leave `CIE D65 at 250 lx` selected.
2. Leave `Selective short-wavelength filter` selected.
3. Choose `Fraction (0–1)` and `Total transmittance`.
4. Confirm the passive, non-fluorescent filter statement.
5. Press `Apply transmission filter`.
6. Compare D65 properties, incident/transmitted absolute values, and the
   action-factor/DER change table.
7. Change the scale to `Percent` and verify that the applied result becomes
   visibly out of date until Apply is pressed again.
8. Select `Zero spectrum` and Apply to inspect undefined-denominator warnings.
