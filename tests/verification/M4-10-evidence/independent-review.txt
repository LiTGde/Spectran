Change under review
The owner identifies only the lightbox detail controls and reduced phone margins
as changed: Originalgroesse / Original size toggles a locally scrollable image;
turning it off restores the fitted overview. Header/footer close controls remain
outside the image viewport. No new asset or scientific-content change was part
of this contract.

Focused cases and observed results
DE, 320 x 844, Lichtpfad:
- The fitted image uses more of the available width and remains fully visible.
- Tab reaches the native Originalgroesse checkbox; Space activates it.
- Original size exposes readable interface and chart detail.
- The named image area receives keyboard focus. Right and Down move only the
  image contents; the dialog header, toggle and close controls remain in place.
- Tab moves from image to footer close and wraps to the cross. Shift+Tab wraps
  in the reverse direction. Focus does not escape to the underlying page.
- Keyboard switching back restores the complete fitted image after panning.
- Escape closes the viewer and restores focus to the Lichtpfad thumbnail.

EN, 390 x 844, Material selection:
- The fitted overview and Original size checkbox are visible and legible.
- Mouse activation reveals readable material-card detail.
- Horizontal and vertical scrolling stays inside the image area, including at
  its lower extent. Header and footer close controls stay reachable.
- Footer Close returns focus to the correct Material selection thumbnail.

EN, 390 x 844, Table Export:
- Enter opens the correct viewer in the same tab. The different image starts
  in its fitted overview, independent of the material image's detail setting.
- Space activates Original size, making row text readable.
- The image area accepts keyboard focus and horizontal arrow-key movement.
  Scrolling reaches the distant right-hand columns without page-wide overflow.
- Tab cycles between the image region, footer close and cross inside the dialog.
- Escape from detail mode restores focus to Table Export.
- Reopening, switching back to fit and closing with the cross all work; focus
  again returns to Table Export. No additional browser tab is created.

These checks resolve the original concern: opening a preview on a phone now
offers usable detail access while preserving an immediate overview and clear
exit controls. The correction introduces no observed issue in the bounded check.
