## Scope

This focused revision addresses the remaining `M4-UX-R31` overlap between a
three-line English archived title and its semantic subtitle at 320 and 390 CSS
px. Scientific calculations, result data, title text, safe right padding,
plot layers, current results, and PNG export behavior are unchanged.

## Change

- Narrow archived plots continue to measure the wrapped result title's actual
  line count.
- An archived title with three or more lines now receives a 12-point bottom
  margin before the semantic subtitle.
- One-line and two-line archived titles receive no additional margin, avoiding
  unnecessary empty space for short names.
- The accepted four-line German archive spacing remains unchanged.
- Adaptive spacing remains limited to archived in-app result plots. Current
  result plots and PNG exports retain the accepted layout.
